## About

I Ching: Yi Jing Life Guide is a modern learning studio for anyone who wants to truly understand the 64 hexagrams—and practice the logic of change, not just read about it. It connects classical text, visual systems, structured lessons, and active recall so you can move from curiosity to confidence.

## Highlights

- **Complete reference**: Read Image, Judgment, and line texts for every hexagram with quick switching between English, Traditional Chinese, and Simplified Chinese.
- **Takashima Ekidan**: Includes 800 hexagram interpretation case studies.
- **Change network (64×64)**: Explore all 4,096 “Original → Changed” combinations, spot moving lines instantly, and understand transformation patterns at a glance.
- **Color-coded 3D globe**: Rotate a hexagram sphere mapped to the eight trigrams (Heaven, Earth, Water, Fire, Mountain, Lake, Thunder, Wind) with distinct colors—customizable to your study style.
- **Structured lessons**: Curated markdown lessons organized by course and section with progress tracking.
- **Interactive quizzes**: Drill names, imagery, Judgment, and line statements with fast feedback.
- **Favorites & notes**: Save any hexagram or change result, write your interpretation, and revisit it anytime.
- **Home Screen widgets**: Pin a hexagram pair or a Yi Lin excerpt for daily reminders and contextual insight.
- **Yi Lin reading**: Connect poetic imagery with real-life situations to deepen understanding.
