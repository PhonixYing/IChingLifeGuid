More than a fortune-telling app ​

I Ching: Yi Jing Life Guide brings AI interpretation, classical text reading, hexagram study, change networks, courses, quizzes, notes, and widgets into one focused app for the Book of Changes.

Use it when you need reflection before a decision. Use it when you want to study the 64 hexagrams seriously. Use it when you want more than a quick answer and less than a dry reference database.

If you are looking for an I Ching app, Yi Jing app, Book of Changes app, hexagram interpretation tool, or I Ching AI reader, this app is designed to help you understand the structure behind the reading, not just receive a vague prediction.

What the app does ​

  • AI interpretation: Turn a question into a structured reading of the hexagram, moving lines, and changed pattern.
  • Multiple casting methods: Practice Da Yan yarrow, three coins, one-line casting, and Takashima-style casting.
  • Classical reading: Study key I Ching texts and commentaries in a calm reader built for long sessions.
  • 64 hexagram reference: Review Judgment, Image, line texts, trigram structure, and related meanings.
  • 4,096 transformation map: Explore every Original → Changed hexagram combination.
  • Courses, quizzes, favorites, and notes: Build long-term understanding instead of relying on one-time consultations.

Who it is for ​

  • People using the I Ching for reflection, decisions, relationships, and timing
  • Beginners learning the 64 hexagrams for the first time
  • Long-time students comparing classical readings and change patterns
  • Readers who want original texts and AI explanation in one place
  • Anyone who wants a quieter, more serious alternative to a simple fortune-telling app

FAQ ​

Is this a fortune-telling app? ​

It can cast and interpret hexagrams, but it is built as a study and reflection tool. The goal is to help you understand a situation through the language of the I Ching.

Can beginners use it? ​

Yes. The app includes AI explanation, lessons, quizzes, and clear hexagram references so you can start without prior knowledge.

Is it useful for advanced students? ​

Yes. Classical texts, 4,096 change patterns, Yi Lin materials, Takashima readings, and reading tools make it useful for deeper study.

Does it replace personal judgment? ​

No. The I Ching is best used as a mirror for reflection. The app is for education, personal insight, and study, not financial, medical, legal, or predictive advice.
